Mochi is a little see-through jelly friend with a big appetite. Feed it, watch everything float in its belly, shake it all up and find out what you made.

For kids. No ads, no account, plays offline.

What happens in Mochi's belly?

Eat, drink, shake, and see what you made. Hundreds of recipes, shapes and moods to find.

Feed and drink

Apples, pizza, cocoa: tap it and Mochi gobbles it up. Hold it and Mochi slurps it in.

Shake it up

Shake the phone and what's inside mixes: apple and water make apple juice.

Stretch and spin

Pull Mochi and it digests faster. Spin it too much and it gets dizzy.

The book

Everything you find goes in Mochi's book, with badges and three little tasks each day.
